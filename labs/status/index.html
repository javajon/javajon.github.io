What the results mean

This page is generated, not maintained. A scheduled sweep drives a real browser through every lab I have authored, end to end, step by step, and writes what happened. Nothing here is typed in by hand, so it cannot quietly fall out of date.

Passing and Failing are statements about the lab. Everything else is the platform declining to let the lab be tested, which is a different thing and is not counted as a defect. Serving an older version is the one to read carefully: the lab is published and runs perfectly well, but the copy O’Reilly serves is older than this repository. Such a lab can still show a Last passed date, from while the two matched, and that is not a contradiction.

A result whose cause lies outside the repository is retried on the next sweep without waiting for anyone to commit anything. Nobody is going to push a fix for an instance that failed to initialize, so the diff alone would never select that lab again and a transient result would stand until the weekly full sweep.

Read a result next to Lab updated. "Passed three weeks ago and untouched since" is reassuring; "passed three weeks ago and rewritten yesterday" is not.
