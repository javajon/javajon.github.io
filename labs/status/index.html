What the results mean

This page is generated, not maintained. A scheduled sweep drives a real browser through every published lab end to end, step by step, and writes what happened. Nothing here is typed in by hand, so it cannot quietly fall out of date.

Passing and Failing are statements about the lab. Everything else is the platform declining to let the lab be tested, which is a different thing and is not counted as a defect. Awaiting publish in particular is routine: the source repository has moved ahead and the served lab has not rebuilt yet.

Read a result next to Lab updated. "Passed three weeks ago and untouched since" is reassuring; "passed three weeks ago and rewritten yesterday" is not.
