How to read this

A suggestion only ever appears within the same shape of tag: 1.29-alpine may be offered 1.30-alpine, never latest or a bare 1.30. A lab that teaches a specific tag style should keep teaching it.

Pinned on purpose means the old version is the lesson - a rolling update needs something to roll from, and a broken image is sometimes the point of the exercise. Those rows carry the reason, and updating them would break the lab they belong to.
